# Chalk & Dynasty: owner's taste

- A basketball game with a quick, satisfying loop; avoid dashboards and form-heavy tools.
- Court, jerseys, ball, scoreboard and a clear Play action dominate.
- Teach one small basketball idea through playing; optional introduction, never homework.
- Natural screens from drawn routes, off-ball cuts and automatic pass timing.
- Ball handler and shooter always under the player's control, even with automatic passes.
- Short sessions, quick retries, five genuinely beatable opponents; skill should matter.
- Broad historical roster, one player at each position, real sourced single-season facts.
- Clear routes preserves starting spots; full reset restores them. Neither loses the team.
- Preserve the existing free public Cloudflare game and its saved progress where possible.
