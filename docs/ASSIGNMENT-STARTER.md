## Introduction starter

For this project, I created Chalk & Dynasty, a browser-based basketball strategy game.
Players draft a five-player lineup from historical NBA team-and-decade pools, draw
movement routes and passing sequences, and try to score against five increasingly
challenging defenses. The project combines my interest in basketball with an exploration
of what it takes to turn a natural-language idea into a tested, playable game.

Public game link: https://chalk-and-dynasty.tyhoag.workers.dev

  ask the coding assistant to make? What did you learn from checking the results?
